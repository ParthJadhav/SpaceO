## Owner direction and qualification limits

The owner requested fixes for the open user issues, a new release, deployment and reporter
replies, and authorized use of this computer without follow-up questions. The
[release policy](../RELEASE_POLICY.md#current-status) records that direction as a 1.0.6-specific
GO with a qualification exception, conditional on source, CI, signing, notarization, immutable
candidate and distribution checks. Those checks passed through the normal protected approvals.

**No new live display/input or exact-artifact behavior qualification was performed. RA-057
remains open.** This Mac's read-only admission check refused a recent WindowServer diagnostic;
no live workload followed that refusal. The WindowServer/ColorSync freeze is unresolved.
Distribution verification establishes artifact authenticity and packaging integrity, and does
not replace the missing runtime qualification. These limits were in the notes at publication.
The exception applies only to 1.0.6; future releases retain the default qualification gates.
