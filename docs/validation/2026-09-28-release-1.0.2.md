## Viewer qualification hold

The first signed native Metal diagnostic failed its required sustained-frame check and is not
passing qualification evidence. Its cleanup restored topology; resource samplers reported no
failure. The fixture completed 900 GPU submissions with zero failures, but reported its window
occluded. Viewer consumers stopped and restarted capture as expected, while sustained visible
frame delivery remained absent.

A separate focused inspection of the signed Viewer confirmed the correct `release-viewer`
session selected, but its canvas showed an external authentication surface instead of the
owned TextEdit document. The diagnostic was stopped for cleanup without attempting control or
bypassing authentication. Publication remains gated pending normal owner unlock and completion
of the exact-candidate Viewer checks. Earlier passing source/matrix results remain separately
identified; this failed Viewer attempt is not relabeled as a pass.
