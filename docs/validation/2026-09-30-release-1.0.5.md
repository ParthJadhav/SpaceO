SpaceO [1.0.5](https://github.com/ParthJadhav/SpaceO/releases/tag/v1.0.5) was published as the
latest full release on September 30, 2026 at 10:40:02 UTC. Its five public downloads match the

[RELEASE_NOTES_1.0.5.md](../RELEASE_NOTES_1.0.5.md).

## Owner decision and qualification limits

After the implemented safeguards, 1,701-test verification result, unresolved macOS root cause,
and installation/release hold pending a separate test Mac were disclosed, the release owner
instructed **"do a release"**. The [release policy](../RELEASE_POLICY.md#current-status) records
that instruction as a 1.0.5-specific GO with a qualification exception, conditional on source,
signing, notarization, and distribution checks passing.

**No new live display/input, sustained memory/Viewer, or exact-artifact behavior qualification
was performed for 1.0.5. RA-057 remains open.** The WindowServer/ColorSync freeze's initiating
cause is unresolved; the safeguards do not establish that another freeze cannot happen.
Previous bounded zero-leak observations do not qualify the changed runtime. The limitation was
present in the public notes at publication. Future releases retain the default qualification
gates. The affected daily-use Mac remains excluded from further display stress work.

The immutable 1.0.4 tag and candidate remain unchanged and unpublished. See the
[freeze investigation](2026-09-29-freeze-investigation.md) for evidence and causal limits.
