## Publication hold after review

Publication status was corrected first to prerelease, then to draft, without changing the tag or
any asset bytes. The public release page and public assets were withdrawn together; the candidate
was retained privately during that interval. The passing results
above remain valid for their stated coverage; they do not close these outstanding items:

- The automation's captured-pointer movement and remote mouse postcondition remain unconfirmed.
  A further right-click diagnostic identified TextEdit as the destination but did not establish
  a visible context-menu result. A physical operator check was requested; no result is assumed.
- The Chromium identity soak diagnostic remains open in `TICKETS.md`: discovery failed closed,
  teardown was retained for recovery, and normal shutdown recovered the host. The passing matrix
  does not establish the root cause or satisfy that ticket's fresh-soak acceptance condition.
- Release approval was delegated under the owner's standing instruction. It must not be described
  as a separate owner review of these completed results. The owner explicitly delegated release work and instructed the implementer not to seek repeated
  permission; that direction remains authorization, not evidence that the technical checks passed.

The implementer returned 1.0.3 to draft and restored 1.0.1 as latest after finding these gaps.
At that point, public publication was placed on HOLD pending completed pointer evidence and
resolution/requalification of the Chromium diagnostic. No failed run is converted into a pass, and no safety control or
private API restriction is relaxed.


## Explicit owner decision after the hold

After receiving the report that 1.0.3 was a signed draft held for missing pointer evidence and the
unresolved Chromium diagnostic, the release owner explicitly instructed: “Yep, release it”.
This is a release-specific owner exception to the outstanding qualification gates, not a claim
that those gates passed. It supersedes the publication HOLD for these exact retained assets.
The pointer evidence and Chromium investigation remain incomplete and remain disclosed in the
public release notes. Failed diagnostics and all earlier results remain unchanged.

Republish the retained five assets as a full release and latest, using the existing immutable
`v1.0.3` tag and source commit. No rebuild, tag movement, permission weakening or runtime safety
change is authorized or needed by this publication step.
